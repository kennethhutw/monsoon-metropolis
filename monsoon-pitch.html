How it works

One invoice, one verified path.

01

Verify

Exporter and buyer hold role-matched Cleanverse credentials (CVI).

02

Tokenize

The invoice is minted as an NFT, hash-locked so it can never be financed twice.

03

Confirm

The named buyer confirms the receivable on-chain.

04

Review

Bank gets an AI assessment, a news scan and a fail-closed AML screen.

05

Settle

Funds disburse in A-Token, a Travel Rule record is written, repayment releases the NFT.

Enforced in the contracts — minting, transfers and financing submission revert without valid CVI.
